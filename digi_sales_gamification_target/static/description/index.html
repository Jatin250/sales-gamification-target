Sales Team Gamification: Targets & Rewards

Boost your sales team's performance with real-time target tracking and visual progress bars directly on Sales Orders.

Key Features

  • Flexible Targets: Set Monthly, Yearly, or Custom date targets for individual salespersons.
  • Real-Time Motivation: A dynamic progress bar appears on Quotations and Sales Orders.
  • Role-Based Security: Only Sales Managers and Administrators can configure targets, preventing unauthorized changes.
  • Automated Math: Instantly calculates confirmed sales against the target window.

Managers can easily configure flexible targets

Motivate Your Team on Every Deal

Salespeople no longer need to guess how close they are to their goal. Whenever a salesperson creates a new Quotation, a clean visual banner displays their current progress.

Once the sale is confirmed, the progress bar updates automatically, providing an instant dopamine hit and driving them to close the next deal!
